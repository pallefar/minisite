Initial probe (false alarm) was 7/10
------------------------------------
First probe of this signal reported 3 healthz returning 401 INVALID_JWT_FORMAT
(grandfathered-policy-notice, privacy-optout-process, request-refund). Investigation
revealed the failure was NOT in the Fns but in the smoke-probe:

  Cause: .env.local SUPABASE_ANON_KEY had trailing `\r` (Windows line endings).
  My initial extraction: `grep ^SUPABASE_ANON_KEY= .env.local | cut -d= -f2-`
  → kept the trailing \r, ANON length=210 instead of 208.
  → 3 Fns rejected the malformed bearer with INVALID_JWT_FORMAT; 7 didn't (either
    luck of header parsing or stricter validation pipeline upstream).
  Fix: pipe through `tr -d '\r\n"'` → ANON length=208 → all 10 succeed.

Lesson: ANY .env.local read in CI / smoke scripts MUST strip \r\n. Surfaced as
candidate memory pattern.

into watch dashboard.
