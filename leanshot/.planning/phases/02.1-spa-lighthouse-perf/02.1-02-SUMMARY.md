## Pivot context

Wave 1's manualChunks fix alone did NOT lift Performance — it stayed at ~0.76 because the actual cold-load bottleneck was `vendor-telemetry` (93 kB gz of @sentry/* + posthog-js) being auto-preloaded due to static imports in `main.tsx` + `analytics.ts`. The fix was to defer telemetry init out of the entry static graph (commit `bf517f6`) which lifted Performance to 0.83. The font CSS render-blocking fix (commit `7ea9a9d`) lifted Performance the rest of the way to 0.94.

Neither of those fixes is what 02.1-02 was scoped to do, but the outcome is the same: Performance ≥ 0.90 was reached without ever needing manual modulepreload injection.
