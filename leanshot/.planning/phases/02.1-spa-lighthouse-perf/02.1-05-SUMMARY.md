## Pivot context (the plan-vs-reality mismatch)

The original 5-wave playbook was scoped to fix bundle topology under the hypothesis that `react-dom` had collapsed back into `index`. Wave 1 fixed that, but the SPA's Lighthouse Performance score was UNCHANGED at ~0.76. TBT was 17-70ms throughout — script execution was never the bottleneck. The hidden cost was network: `vendor-telemetry` (93 kB gz) was auto-preloaded due to static `import * as Sentry` in `main.tsx` and `import posthog` in `analytics.ts`.

Recorded in commit messages on `bf517f6` (telemetry defer) and `7ea9a9d` (font CSS non-blocking) for reproducibility. Cross-referenced from `02.1-BUNDLE-MEASUREMENT.md` "Why each lever moved (or didn't move) the needle".
