## Bundle-ceiling raise sequencing

Per RESEARCH Finding 10: current `CLINIC_CEILING=36000` (set by Phase 30 verifier orphan-fix). Estimated P31 net add: ~12.5 kB gz (BrandingTab ~5 + OnboardingTab ~6 + RoleEditorModal expansion ~1.5; SortableTreePanel extraction is net-zero because page-builder chunk loses ~1.5 kB while clinic gains ~1.5 kB). Projected: ~48.5 kB gz before optimization. Target ceiling: **50,000 bytes gz** (~1.5 kB headroom).

**Owner: Plan 31-01 owns the ceiling raise as its FIRST task** (`CLINIC_CEILING=50000` in `scripts/assert-clinic-bundle-budget.sh`).
