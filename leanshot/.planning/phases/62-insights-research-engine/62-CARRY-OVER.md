## INTENTIONAL DEVIATION from CONTEXT.md

`purge_research_data_for_revoked()` implements **UPDATE `last_purged_at` + matview `WHERE research_consent = true` filter** rather than the locked CONTEXT.md decision ("deletes from every `insights_*_source` source table").

**Rationale:** Source tables (`injections`, `weights`, `ai_messages`) are user's own dose-log data, not research-exclusive. Deleting from them would corrupt the user's own primary data. The matview WHERE clause approach achieves the same observable outcome (revoked user's data does not appear in future research rollups) without destroying user-owned data. 24h SLA still met (matview refresh runs nightly at 02:00 UTC; consent revoke → last_purged_at update → next refresh skips user).
