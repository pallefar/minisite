Ship the 3 embed-provider blocks — Calendly, YouTube, Tally — as three SEPARATE draggable
blocks (D-01, D-02), each with its own tailored property editor, plus their three
`renderBlock()` iframe branches in the public `page-render` renderer.

3 embed blocks. It thickens the MVP slice produced by Wave 2 (15-03 backend + 15-04 editor

tune it in the right rail, publish, and a visitor sees a real sandboxed iframe with a
loading skeleton.

  the validate+build helpers for the 3 iframe `src` strings AND the full per-provider
