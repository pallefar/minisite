### Plan 65.1-02 (close-out, this commit)

**Sequential drift fixes** applied inline to unblock the full 18-migration push:

1. **Phase 66.5 PG-version drift fix** (`supabase/migrations/20290106000002_security_definer_view_refactor.sql`):
   - `ALTER MATERIALIZED VIEW ... SET (security_invoker = on)` requires PG ≥ 16; LeanShot remote is older.
