## D-31 (AMENDED) — W-9 / 1099-NEC threshold

**Original D-31:**
> **$500 W-9 threshold strict:** affiliate accrued commission held until `SUM(commission_cents) >= 50000`. On reach, Stripe Connect auto-prompts W-9/W-8BEN. 1099-NEC auto-generated by Stripe at year-end.

**Amendment:** The $500 number is **correct and intentional**, but the rationale ("Stripe Connect default") was wrong — Stripe has **no default**; platforms set their own enforcement thresholds. Restated rationale:

> **$500 W-9 threshold (deliberate fraud-reduction policy).** We collect tax info well below the 2026 IRS legal $2,000 threshold (raised from $600 by OBBB Act) to:
> 1. Front-load identity verification — fraudulent affiliates abandon when asked for W-9.
> 2. Avoid year-end surprises — affiliates know upfront a 1099 is coming.
> 3. Maintain a paper trail for chargeback disputes.
>
> **Implementation:** `affiliates.tax_threshold_cents = 50000` configurable column (default 50000) so we can adjust later without migration. Stripe Connect's `account_link` flow surfaces hosted W-9/W-8BEN forms when our application calls `stripe.accounts.update({ requirements: { ... } })` triggering tax-form collection — Stripe does NOT auto-prompt; we trigger.

## D-36 (NEW) — Renewals do NOT count as conversions

> **Single-tier $10 flat applies to INITIAL paid conversion only.** Subscription renewals do not generate new affiliate commission rows.
>
> **Implementation:** `stripe-webhook` filters `invoice.paid` events where `invoice.billing_reason = 'subscription_create'` (initial) AND `invoice.billing_reason != 'subscription_cycle'` (renewal). Trial-to-paid transitions fire `subscription_create` once when the first paid invoice settles — that's the conversion moment.
>
> **Why:** Matches CONTEXT D-08 "single-tier $10 flat per paid conversion". Bounded total commission liability per converted user. Simpler ledger; no per-renewal state machine.
>
> **Test:** Two scenarios — (a) trial → first paid invoice writes one `affiliate_conversions` row; (b) renewal invoice writes ZERO new rows. Both assertions in `affiliate-attribution.test.ts`.
